01

A focused gold producer
in Shandong Province

From a single open-pit operation in 2011 to a listed group with two producing mines and three development-stage underground mines, we grow by operating efficiently, acquiring selectively, and mining responsibly.

Persistence Gold Group Ltd (formerly Persistence Resources Group Ltd) was incorporated in the Cayman Islands on 21 May 2019. Through Majestic Yantai Gold Ltd (BVI), the Group holds 75% of Yantai Zhongjia Mining — operator of the Songjiagou open-pit and underground mines — alongside partner Yantai Dahedong Mineral Processing.

On 1 March 2025 the Group completed the acquisition of a 52% controlling interest in Yantai Mujin Mining, adding the Denggezhuang, Chahe and Heiniutai underground mines and materially increasing the Group's high-grade resource base.
